BLUE PRINCE VR 0.1.96 - BUTTONS / PHYSICAL STUFF
October 25, 2026

Default right-handed setup. Normal menu/tool roles follow comfort
handedness. Physical X/Y shortcuts and editor voice stay LEFT; physical B
and reading-photo shutter stay RIGHT.

GETTING AROUND
Left stick: move. Click for slow walking.
Right stick: turn/look. Click to crouch.
Trigger: ordinary click/interact. Grip: grab/hold.
A: confirm (X in left-handed menu mode). Tap B: Back.
Hold physical B for ONE SECOND in ordinary play: pause/day-ending menu.
Inside menus, documents and terminals, B backs out immediately.
In normal room play: physical X is keyboard X; physical Y is E/use-item.
Y is blocked during typing, drafting, editing and VR settings.

VR OPTIONS
Select VR OPTIONS below Quit on the title screen. X is still a shortcut.
In play, hold both thumbstick clicks. Graphics cycles Performance,
Balanced and Quality. Restart after changing resolution. This does not
change headset refresh or enable/disable runtime Spacewarp.

BAG / BLUEPRINT
Grip behind RIGHT side: bag. Grip behind LEFT side: rolled blueprint.
Either hand can reach either location. Release grip before a new draw.
Reach/grip a bag slot for camera, photos, notebook, recorder or blueprint.
Bring the blueprint forward to unroll it.
ONE X tap flips it fully between map and the native menu on its back.
Use the stick/A on a ready page; nothing confirms ending the day for you.
Bring the map behind your LEFT side to roll/store it automatically.
No prior B press or grip release is needed for the held map's return.
B optionally rolls/unrolls the held map and can cancel a pending flip.
Other tools/gadgets use their grip/reach/release back-stow gesture.
The native map appears automatically while drafting rooms.

DOORS / TOOLS
Right grip near a door knob, then twist. Lever handle: grip/push down.
Shovel/Power Shovel/Jack Hammer: touch dirt mound with the blade.
Right hand on shovel top grip; left can grip shaft for support.
Jack Hammer replaces shovel and uses native upgraded digging rewards.
It does NOT break walls. One successful dig contact requests the reward.
Hammer/Morning Star: swing the head into supported chests/vases.
Power Hammer also works on eligible native weak walls/structures.
Chest key: available keys + empty tool hand, approach and poke latch
with the right-hand pinched key. Confirm native key prompt normally.
Pull away to retry after cancelling. Costs and loot stay game-owned.

ROOM INTERACTIONS
Safes, terminals, utility/garage buttons: touch with a fingertip.
Lift off between presses. Safe Enter and Clear are included.
Pump: grip handle and move up/down. Boiler wheels/gas knobs: grip/turn.
Small switches: pinch; supported sliders: grip/move along their track.
Wall/Lab levers: grip/pull. Secret books: grip spine/pull.
Room 8 statues: grip, carry, place in supported native slots.
Parlor: owned Wind-up Key appears near boxes; poke box/key area.
Watering can: owned can auto-equips in supported green rooms; tilt/pour.
Handheld telescope: owned scope auto-equips in Observatory; eye-align 4s.
Large Observatory telescope: grip supported controls and move them.
Railroad cart: trigger hold; release trigger to let go. Back cancels.
The cart release fix also handles tracking/focus loss. Releasing is
required before grabbing again after cancellation.
Safehouse boat: aim/reach with either hand and press trigger. The native
boat must actually be available; the mod doesn't bypass dock unlocks.
Forklift: existing trigger/grip drive and lift controls remain separate.

CAMERA / READING
Grab Camera from bag. Holding-hand trigger: shutter.
Shake physical print to develop. Shoot again after print motor finishes.
Camera stays in hand when grip is released; back-stow to store it.
Camera and print can be put away independently. X is fallback camera close.
Book/document: X enters photo mode; physical RIGHT trigger shoots;
X returns to reading. Flash and shutter sound still play.
Outside photo mode, right trigger handles pages. With Magnifying Glass
owned, left stick moves it automatically; no extra activation chord.

PHOTOS / NOTEBOOK / VOICE
Grab Photos or Notebook from bag. X switches pencil/recorder.
Pencil: existing pencil tip/eraser writes/erases.
Voice: recorder in LEFT hand. HOLD LEFT trigger to talk, RELEASE for text.
Photo words go on its bottom white border. Y closes the editor.
Native photo/page browsing remains. Don't quit during recording/saving.
Standalone recorder: select bag slot; holding-hand trigger records.
Back-stow when finished; pending voice processing/saving finishes first.
Saved outside-VR copies: game folder's VR Photos and VR Journal.

OPTIONAL DEVELOPER
PC F: room picker. PC I: item picker. Use a separate save.
Overlay bars are hidden by default; pickers still work.
