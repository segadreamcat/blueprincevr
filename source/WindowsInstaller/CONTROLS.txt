BLUE PRINCE VR - OCTOBER 10 WINDOWS RELEASE

Move: left stick. Turn: right stick. Look: headset.
Walk slowly: left stick click. Crouch: right stick click.
Interact: trigger. Pick up/hold: grip. Use held item: trigger + grip.
Journal/camera/notebook: tap X. Voice memo: hold X.
Floor plan: tap Y. Pause: hold Y. Confirm: A. Back: B.
Forklift / pallet jack: hold trigger to keep hold; grips control lift.
Magnifier: hold grip over a document; left stick moves the glass.
VR options: hold both stick clicks in play, or X on the title screen.
SteamVR bindings may differ. X/Y remain on the left controller when the
dominant hand changes. Smooth/snap turning is selected in VR options.

COMPUTERS
Touch terminal keys with your fingertips. A/B and sticks also operate
Enter, Escape and the arrow menu keys. For recalibration, hold both grips
for three seconds at a computer, then touch Q, P and Z with the right
index tip and press A after each touch. Calibration is shared between
computers; the Security terminal still needs gameplay confirmation.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
Experimental PC VR build. Performance depends on the PC and room.
ResolutionScale and foveation are available for tuning; no FPS improvement
or additional headset compatibility is promised by this packaging pass.
Avoid F6: it generates large diagnostic screenshots in UserData.
For reports include the room, held item, input and MelonLoader\Latest.log.
Check the log for personal paths before posting it publicly.
